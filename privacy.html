StarStable Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the StarStable Discord bot ("StarStable", "the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Bot on Discord.

1. Information We Collect

StarStable may process information that is provided through Discord when you interact with the Bot.

This may include:

  • Your Discord user ID
  • Your Discord server ID
  • Your Discord username or display name
  • Information contained in commands or messages sent to the Bot
  • Bot configuration settings for a Discord server

We only process information that is necessary for the Bot to provide its features and functionality.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by StarStable may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the Bot's features
  • Respond to commands and requests
  • Maintain server-specific Bot settings
  • Prevent abuse or misuse of the Bot
  • Fix errors and improve the Bot

3. Data Storage

Depending on the features being used, certain information may be stored temporarily or persistently so that the Bot can function correctly.

We do not intentionally collect or store sensitive personal information through the Bot.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or intentionally share personal information collected through StarStable with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by third-party services that are required for the operation of the Bot, such as Discord or hosting and database providers.

5. Discord

StarStable operates through Discord. Your use of Discord and interaction with the Bot is also subject to Discord's own terms and privacy policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's functionality or for legitimate operational purposes.

Server administrators may remove the Bot from their Discord server at any time. Where applicable, stored server-specific information may then be deleted.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable privacy laws, you may have rights regarding personal information processed by StarStable. These may include the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact the Bot developer.

8. Children's Privacy

StarStable is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information from users.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page together with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how StarStable handles information, please contact the StarStable developer through the official Discord server or the contact method provided by the developer.

11. Acceptance

By using StarStable, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
